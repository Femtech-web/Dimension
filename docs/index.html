Moonwell MIP-X43

MIP-X43 executed at Base block 42,194,663 and was undone at block 42,538,949. In between, Moonwell valued cbETH at the cbETH/ETH exchange rate. Dimension's oracle reads the same two feeds and multiplies them, as the types require.
